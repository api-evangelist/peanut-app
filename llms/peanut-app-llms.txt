# Peanut

> The ultimate app connecting women through all stages of womanhood—from fertility and pregnancy to motherhood and menopause.

Peanut is a social networking app designed to reduce feelings of isolation and empower women to connect worldwide. Founded in 2017 by Michelle Kennedy, Peanut provides a safe space for women to find friends, ask questions, and receive support from others at similar life stages. With over 5 million trusted users and a 4.4-star rating, Peanut makes it easy to discover like-minded women nearby who share your interests, hobbies, and journey.

Key features and benefits:
- Swipe to discover and connect with women in your area based on life stage and interests
- Join groups and communities aligned with your journey, from local meet-ups to shared interests
- Ask questions and get real advice from women who understand your experiences
- Access expert articles and resources at your fingertips
- Safe, moderated space with community guidelines to ensure respectful interactions

## Life Stage Communities

- [Fertility Community](https://peanut-app.io/fertility-community): Connect with others trying to conceive, share TTC experiences, and find support during your fertility journey
- [Pregnancy Community](https://peanut-app.io/pregnancy-community): Discover advice, tips, and information from other mamas-to-be throughout your pregnancy
- [Motherhood Community](https://peanut-app.io/motherhood-community): Meet mamas nearby with similarly aged children and build lasting friendships
- [Menopause Community](https://peanut-app.io/menopause-community): Share your menopause journey, find answers to questions, and access support groups

## Features

- Discovery: Swipe-based interface to find women nearby who match your interests and life stage
- Groups: Explore and join communities that align with your journey and interests
- Community Posts: Ask questions, share stories, and get valuable advice from real women
- Audio Chat: Connect through voice conversations with other members
- Direct Messaging: Build one-on-one connections with women you meet on the app
- Expert Content: Access articles and resources written by professionals and experienced mamas
